# <meta name="robots" content="noindex"> tag, which keeps the page out of
# search results. (Disallowing them here would hide the noindex and could
# leave bare URLs in results instead.)
#
# AI crawlers ignore the noindex tag, so they read the full pages — which is
# what we want.
